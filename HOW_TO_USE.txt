HOW TO USE: idiolect-phonetic-atlas
====================================

PURPOSE
  Catalog and formalize a speaker's private phoneme system, Wells 24 lexical sets,
  allophonic rules, VOT in ms, rhoticity %, th-stopping rate, yod-dropping,
  idiosyncratic word pronunciations, and minimal pairs for voice cloning and synthesis.

CAPABILITIES
  - Map steady-state formant targets (F1/F2/F3) and duration for Wells 24 lexical sets
  - Measure Voice Onset Time (VOT ms) and release bursts across stop categories
  - Quantify sociolinguistic indices (rhoticity %, th-stopping, yod-dropping, short-a split)
  - Curate idiosyncratic word pronunciation dictionary (e.g. huge, coffee, water)
  - Generate phonemic minimal-pair discrimination checklist (cot/caught, pin/pen)
  - Output machine-readable idiolect_atlas.json

USE CASES
  - "Give me the exact phoneme breakdown of this speaker beyond generic NYC accent"
  - You are training or conditioning a voice clone and need an IPA pronunciation dictionary
  - You want to verify whether a voice clone preserves the speaker's distinct minimal pairs
  - You need to determine why two voices with identical pitch still sound different

IDEAS
  - Pair with prosodic-grammar-intonation-os to combine tune with phoneme realization
  - Feed idiolect_atlas.json directly into synthesis-control-compiler for Kokoro/SSML
  - Use the minimal pair checklist in clone-verification-match-score

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  /idiolect-atlas Queens-male-orator

  create phonetic atlas for Steve Jobs

  idiolect atlas of a 30yo male with Down syndrome

  map phoneme system for this speaker. Include all 24 Wells lexical sets and VOT.
